About

I’m a Data & Technology Consultant. I make numbers trustworthy: auditing reporting estates, root causing data quality failures and working out what a company’s existing stack can actually do. I also ship the layers underneath, from pipelines to production services.

Most recently I held an independent retainer with Knoops, a premium hot chocolate brand scaling across 30+ UK stores (March to July 2026). I owned their Power BI report layer and audited an estate whose flagship Weekly Scorecard carried 600+ measures, led incident response on a Shopify migration that contaminated UK order data, assessed their SaaS stack (Klaviyo, Shopify, Revel, Arch) against actual usage, and briefed the CEO and CFO directly.

Before consulting I was a Data Science Analyst Intern at Icon Life Sciences, building a BigQuery golden source and brand-level forecasts (MAPE 7.9%), and worked on CNN/OCR microservices at scale (p95 < 300 ms @ ~1k req/s).

I’m based in Dublin. Outside of tech, I co-founded FarmEasy, a backyard-to-table micro-farm, and I’ve served as treasurer for student societies, managing ≈ €25,000.
